What it does

🔔

Basic reminders

One date, two pings — 24h and 2h before the deadline. Too close to fit both? You still get one ping, right at the deadline.

🔁

Monthly reminders

Give it a day of the month and it repeats — 48h and 24h ahead, then 09:00 on the day itself, until you tap Stop repeating.

📝

Quick notes

No date needed — just text. A grocery list or anything you don't want to forget, nudged every 2 hours until you close it.

🌙

Quiet hours

No pings between 22:00 and 08:00 your time. Anything due overnight waits and arrives first thing in the morning instead.

How it works

  1. 1

    Pick a type

    Tap ➕ New reminder in @lviv_reminder_bot and choose Basic, Monthly, or Note.

  2. 2

    Send one line

    note @ Month Day HH:MM for a date, note @ Day for monthly, or just plain text for a note.

  3. 3

    Get pinged on schedule

    The bot handles the offsets and quiet hours. Tap Close (or Stop repeating) whenever you're done.

Heads-up: works in English and Ukrainian — switch anytime with /language. Your timezone is set with /timezone.

Free & open source

Reminder Bot is a small Python bot built on python-telegram-bot and SQLite. Read the code, run your own instance, or open an issue.
